Music player for Windows

Doodley Music

A free music player where a pixel character named Doodle lives in a cosy room and dances to whatever you play.

Doodle studies at the desk when it's quiet, sleeps at night as the room follows your clock, and a cat drops by now and then. You can drop in your own music files or paste a link to save a song with its cover art.

I designed the whole look myself: three styles (Doodle, Studio Glass and Record Shop), each in light and dark, plus a vinyl record you can drag to scratch.

In-game notes overlay

Nepnote

Press Shift + F1 in a game and your notes, checklists and boss strategies appear right on top. Press it again and they're gone.

Each game gets its own notebook with folders, colours and game-themed icons. Notes can be plain text or tickable checklists, and you can pin one as a small card while you play.

It's a tiny 3 MB app built to stay out of anti-cheat's way: it never touches the game and steps aside when anti-cheat is running.
